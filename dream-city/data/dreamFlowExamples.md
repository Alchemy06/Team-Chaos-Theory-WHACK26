# Connected database demo dreams

Ten fictional completed journals. Three school/exam dreams, three ocean/house dreams, two flying dreams, and two standalone dreams. Each has three answers. Dates differ so recurring-dream links can form under the existing rules. The UI retains its original three prompts.

- The Exam That Would Not Start — apprehension
- The Paper Classroom — wonder
- The Unfair Exam — anger
- The House in the Tide — sadness
- The Quiet Floating House — happiness
- The House Below the Ocean — confusion
- A Flight Above the Clouds — wonder
- The Familiar Flight — happiness
- The Silver Smile — confusion
- The Ember Sculpture — happiness
